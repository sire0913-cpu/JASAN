Privacy Policy (English)

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Bunker Defense (package com.bunker.defense.global, the "App"), published by AR Craft ("we", "us"), handles information. We designed the App to collect as little as possible.

1. Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store on our own servers, sell or rent any personal information. The App has no account system, no advertising and no analytics SDK.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (wave, resources, upgrades, settings, language choice, best score and which premium unlocks you own) is saved only in the App's local storage on your device. It is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

3. Third-party services provided by Google

The App uses the following Google services. When you use them, Google processes data under the Google Privacy Policy.

ServicePurposeData involved
Google Play Games ServicesSign-in and leaderboards (daily, weekly, monthly, all-time)Your Play Games player ID, gamer name/avatar and the scores you submit. Scores are visible to other players on the leaderboards.
Google Play BillingOptional one-time in-app purchases (premium unlocks)Purchase records and payment details are handled by Google. We only receive confirmation of which item you own; we never see your payment information.
Google Play In-App UpdatesChecking for and installing required updatesApp version and update status handled by Google Play.

4. Sharing

We do not share personal information with anyone. Data handled by Google services above is shared only between you and Google as described in Google's policies.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your choices

  • You can play without submitting scores by not signing in to Google Play Games.
  • You can delete local game data by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in Android settings.
  • You can manage or delete your Play Games profile and data in your Google account settings.

7. Security

Because we do not collect personal data on our servers, no such data is held by us. Purchases and sign-in use Google's secure systems.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: artkasset@gmail.com
